Two-point map ruler

Select the ruler below Layers, then click or tap two points. Move the pointer to preview. Drag either completed endpoint to adjust the line and distance; a third ordinary click starts another measurement.

Straight-line geographic distance, in m or km. This is not a route or terrain-surface distance. Drag and pinch away from endpoints still navigate. Reset starts over; Escape or Exit clears the ruler. No location permission or saved features.
