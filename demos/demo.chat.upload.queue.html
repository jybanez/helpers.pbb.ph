Integration responsibilities

Your app owns selection, limits, authorization, transport, retries and preview URLs. This component only renders the list and emits callbacks. Progress-only updates preserve the bar and audio player; changing attachment content rebuilds previews.

Image preview URLs must be images. Video preview URLs must be playable video; optional thumbUrl/posterUrl must be images. Audio preview URLs use the Helper audio player. Unsupported preview formats depend on browser support.

Completion text is omitted inside small media thumbnails. Upload errors remain visible outside thumbnails. Use null progress while the upload total is unknown.
