MapLibre drawing adapter

Choose Line: click its start, move to preview, then click its end to complete. Path adds connected segments; double-click its last endpoint or press Finish. Polygon uses Finish. Cancel discards the draft. Select a line/path and drag any segment to move the whole object; white vertex handles reshape it.

On the focused map, Enter adds a vertex at the map center, Escape cancels, Delete removes the selection and Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes (Shift+Z redoes). Finish also works with its toolbar button by keyboard.

Scope and ownership

This local, tile-free demo uses the complete Helper toolbar and canonical adapter. Measurement, vertex insertion/removal, snapping and topology validation are unsupported. GeoJSON stays in memory. MapLibre assets and drawing code load locally; no sensor, upload or persistence requests are made.

API, dependencies and lifecycle contract

Existing-object edit session

Import sample shapes, select an object, then begin an edit. Drag vertices or apply colors. The complete toolbar Finish accepts one undo step; Cancel restores the object and prior history. While unchanged, Finish/Cancel are disabled: click another object to switch, choose Pan or a drawing mode, or use Delete with confirmation. Changed edits must be finished or cancelled before switching or deleting. Cancelling deletion keeps the clean session. Colors remain in GeoJSON properties.helperStyle.
